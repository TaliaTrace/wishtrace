# Builder Handbook Notes

Source: uploaded Agentic Commerce Hackathon Builder Handbook.

## Non-negotiable takeaways

- Build a working end-to-end experience, not only screens or a deck.
- The product must make sense without a long explanation.
- Prava must enable the core commercial action.
- Narrow and well-built can beat broad and unfinished.
- Strong projects combine real problem, complete action, original insight, trust and product quality.
- Generic chat wrappers, copied RFH ideas, mocked payments and bolted-on partners will not stand out.
- Judging covers end-to-end functionality, novelty, value, Prava, partner tech, product experience and continuation potential.
- SDK/API is recommended for native embedded payment experiences and supports sandbox.
- A payment session alone is not a completed order.
- Stay in sandbox until the complete flow is reliable.
- Temporary production access may be requested during August 1–8 and is not automatic.
- Merchant directories are discovery resources, not guarantees.
- Only registered participants may contribute.
- Judged work must be completed during the official window.
- Pre-existing work must be disclosed.
- Fake transactions or fabricated results may disqualify.
- Judges may request live demo, repository, logs and transaction evidence.
- Submission needs demo, video, repo, Prava explanation, track evidence, disclosure and learning summary.
- Use Birdie and the Prava Discord support channel first.
