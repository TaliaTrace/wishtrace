# RFH Notes

The RFH document says the hackathon is about a working experience where an agent understands intent, finds the right option and completes a purchase, booking, renewal or payment through Prava.

RFHs are starting points rather than specifications. Teams may narrow, combine or create something different.

WishTrace maps most directly to RFH #2: shopping/styling agent, specifically gifts and occasions.

The strongest fit is:

```text
understand recipient + occasion intent
→ find suitable real products
→ make a constrained choice
→ complete the approved transaction through Prava
```

The RFH also states that Prava should be meaningful and not a disconnected checkout at the end.
