# Image Generation Prompts

These prompts are for art direction. Real UI must be recreated in Compose.

## Global direction

Append to every prompt:

```text
Light warm premium Android UI, soft ivory background, restrained lavender and pale blue accents, trustworthy consumer-product quality, generous whitespace, accessible typography, realistic mobile proportions, minimal text, no dark theme, no neon, no glassmorphism overload, no generic AI sparkles, no fake brand logos, no malformed text, no impossible interactions.
```

## Onboarding

```text
Create one 9:20 portrait Android onboarding screen for WishTrace. The product helps busy people remember important occasions and send thoughtful gifts on time. Show a refined WishTrace mark that combines a small gift with a subtle trace/path. Headline: “The right gift, right on time.” Supporting copy: “Remember the people and little hints. WishTrace handles the rest.” Use a compact visual story with Sophie, birthday in 12 days, interests, budget and one selected gift. Primary button: “Add someone you care about.” The screen must feel like real onboarding, not a marketing poster.
```

## Home

```text
Create a realistic Android home screen for WishTrace. The hero is Sophie’s birthday in 12 days, with a warm recipient card, budget and one clear “Find a gift” action. Show two smaller upcoming people below. The screen should feel recipient-first and occasion-first, not like a calendar grid or ecommerce marketplace.
```

## Recipient detail

```text
Create a recipient profile screen for Sophie in WishTrace. Show relationship, next birthday, interests, dislikes, saved hints with dates/sources and budget. Make the evidence feel private and editable. Primary action: “Find a gift.” Avoid contact-app styling and avoid a wall of chips.
```

## Discovery

```text
Create a WishTrace discovery screen where real product candidates are checked against budget, availability and recipient context. Show meaningful stages and three candidate cards. Two candidates visibly recede with factual labels such as “Over budget” and “Arrives too late.” One candidate remains selected. No chat UI and no fake thinking orb.
```

## Purchase review

```text
Create a trustworthy WishTrace purchase review screen. Show Sophie’s birthday, exact product, merchant, variant, amount, currency, delivery fact, budget boundary and personal message. Primary button must state the exact action, such as “Approve $50 purchase.” Include Prava as the secure payment layer without inventing or distorting its logo.
```

## Success

```text
Create a warm WishTrace “Gift secured” screen after verified payment. The selected gift card or product visually resolves into a clean receipt with merchant, amount, reference, delivery status and personal-message status. Use restrained celebratory motion cues, not excessive confetti.
```

## Promotional image

```text
Create a professional landscape launch graphic for WishTrace with one phone, one occasion card, one selected gift and a short flow from person to date to gift to sent. Use official logos only when supplied as source assets. Keep copy under 15 words. The graphic should explain thoughtful gifting on time, not a savings tracker or wishlist.
```
