# Judge Q&A

## What is the problem?

People remember that they care but lose the date, the hint or the time needed to choose and purchase a suitable gift.

## Why is this more than a reminder?

WishTrace discovers real products, makes a constrained decision and completes the approved transaction through Prava.

## What is the agentic part?

It evaluates recipient context and real candidates, removes invalid products, ranks the remaining choices and moves the selected transaction to completion. Deterministic code enforces hard limits.

## Why is OpenAI needed?

Relationship hints are messy and contextual. OpenAI turns them into structured preferences, ranks candidate products against evidence and drafts an editable personal message. It does not control payment facts.

## Why is Prava central?

Without Prava, WishTrace would stop at a recommendation or handle sensitive payment details itself. Prava provides the authorized payment path and transaction result.

## What prevents overspending?

The budget, merchant, product snapshot and exact amount are validated in code before approval. The user sees the exact charge, and idempotency prevents duplicate transactions.

## Why will users return?

They build a small private memory of important people and occasions. Each saved hint and completed gift improves future usefulness.

It enables quick capture, reminders and a polished mobile relationship workflow. The hackathon implementation also targets the best-UX prize.
