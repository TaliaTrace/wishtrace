# Startup Story

## Company sentence

WishTrace is a private occasion and relationship-memory layer that turns scattered context into completed, thoughtful gift purchases.

## Initial customer

Busy students and young professionals who buy for a small circle of close friends, partners and family.

## Current workaround

- calendar reminders;
- screenshots and chat search;
- notes apps;
- retailer browsing;
- generic gift guides;
- last-minute gift cards;
- manual checkout.

The problem is not lack of stores. It is lost context and unfinished intent.

## Differentiation

### Versus reminders

WishTrace completes discovery and purchase.

### Versus wishlists

Recipients do not need to create or maintain a list.

### Versus generic recommenders

Recommendations are grounded in relationship context and explicit constraints.

### Versus retailer recommendations

WishTrace is person-first rather than inventory-first and can eventually span merchants.

## Retention loop

1. Add close people.
2. Save hints naturally over time.
3. Upcoming occasion creates value.
4. Completed gift creates trust.
5. More hints and occasions increase future value.

Potential metric:

- recipients with at least one useful hint;
- active upcoming occasions;
- discovery-to-purchase conversion;
- repeat completed gifts;
- percentage of recommendations accepted without replacement.

## Distribution

- Android share sheet for links/screenshots/text;
- occasion reminders;
- post-purchase word of mouth;
- creator/merchant gifting surfaces;
- university and young-professional communities.

## Monetization

- affiliate or merchant commission on completed purchases;
- premium recipient history and planning;
- premium family/household gifting calendar;
- merchant gifting integration;
- optional concierge tier.

Do not claim agreements that do not exist.

## What the hackathon proves

- people understand the product quickly;
- recipient context improves choice;
- one transaction can be completed safely;
- users trust visible evidence and limits;
- the mobile UX can feel emotional without sacrificing payment clarity.

## 90-day plan

### Month 1

- validate 20–30 users;
- support two reliable merchants;
- implement share-sheet hint capture;
- measure accepted recommendations.

### Month 2

- add delivery/address workflow;
- add repeat occasion planning;
- improve merchant fallback;
- test affiliate economics.

### Month 3

- private beta;
- premium planning experiment;
- merchant/creator partnership outreach;
- production payment onboarding.
